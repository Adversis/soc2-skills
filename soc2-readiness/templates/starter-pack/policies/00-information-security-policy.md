# Information Security Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}  
**Owner:** {{SECURITY_OWNER}}  
**Applies to:** All ACME employees, contractors, and systems

---

## 1. Purpose

ACME AI, Inc. is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the information it holds on behalf of its customers and itself. This policy establishes ACME's information security program and defines the scope of controls that govern how information is collected, used, stored, and protected.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all ACME employees and contractors and to all systems used to deliver or support ACME's services, including the hosted ACME platform and corporate IT systems.

## 3. Program Ownership

The {{SECURITY_OWNER}} is responsible for the information security program, including maintaining these policies, overseeing their implementation, and ensuring that controls remain appropriate to the risks ACME faces. The {{SECURITY_OWNER}} reviews the program at least annually and may initiate an out-of-cycle review in response to significant changes in the business, threat landscape, or regulatory environment.
## 4. Policy Set

ACME's information security program is governed by the following policies:

| Policy | Scope |
|--------|-------|
| Personnel Security | Hiring, training, offboarding, and conduct |
| Risk Assessment | Identification, evaluation, and treatment of information security risks |
| Information Classification | Data classification tiers and handling requirements |
| Vendor Management | Security review and oversight of third-party service providers |
| Incident Response | Detection, containment, investigation, and notification of security incidents |
| Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery | Recovery objectives and continuity planning |
| Access Control | Provisioning, deprovisioning, and review of system access |
| Password and Authentication | Credential requirements and multi-factor authentication |
| Change Management | Review and approval of application and infrastructure changes |
| Testing | Security testing and validation requirements |
| Patch Management | Vulnerability tracking and remediation timelines |
| Data Retention | Retention schedules and secure deletion of customer and operational data |
| Physical Security | Device management and work location controls |
| Encryption | Encryption requirements for data in transit and at rest |
| Acceptable Use | Permitted and prohibited use of company systems and data |

## 5. Acknowledgment and Training

All employees and contractors are required to review and acknowledge these policies at hire and annually thereafter. Security awareness training is provided at hire and annually to reinforce policy requirements and emerging threats.

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Adherence to these policies is a condition of employment and engagement. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Suspected violations should be reported to the {{SECURITY_OWNER}} or to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

## 7. Review

This policy and the policies listed in Section 4 are reviewed at least annually by the {{SECURITY_OWNER}}. Updates are communicated to all employees and require re-acknowledgment when material changes are made.
