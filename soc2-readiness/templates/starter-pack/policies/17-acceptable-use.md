# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** {{SECURITY_OWNER}}  
**Applies to:** All ACME employees and contractors

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the acceptable use of ACME-provided devices, systems, and data. It sets expectations for employee behavior and protects the company, its customers, and its employees.

## 2. Company-Provided Devices

Company-issued devices are primarily for business use. Incidental personal use is acceptable provided it does not consume resources in a way that affects work performance or expose company data to personal accounts or services.

Employees install software from reputable, established sources. Tools that access production systems, handle customer data, or store company credentials require {{SECURITY_OWNER}} awareness before use. No software may disable or circumvent device security controls (MDM, full-disk encryption, screen lock).

Employees must not allow other individuals to use their company device for work purposes.

## 3. Company Systems and Data

Employees may only access ACME systems and data for authorized business purposes. Employees must not:

- Access systems or data beyond what their role requires
- Share credentials with other individuals (see Password Policy and Access Control Policy)
- Copy Restricted data to personal accounts, devices, or unauthorized third-party services
- Use customer data for purposes other than providing the ACME service or debugging a reported issue

## 4. Communications

Company communication platforms (chat and email) should be used professionally. Employees must not use these platforms to transmit:

- Credentials, API keys, or secrets (use the company secrets management system or a password manager)
- Customer data (unless required for a specific, authorized purpose, and then only over encrypted channels within company systems)
- Content that is harassing, discriminatory, or violates applicable law

## 5. AI Tools

Employees who use AI coding assistants or other generative AI tools for work must not:

- Submit customer data to external AI services without {{SECURITY_OWNER}} approval
- Submit proprietary source code to AI services whose terms do not include confidentiality protections. [Approved enterprise tools (business or team tiers of coding assistants whose terms include such protections) may be used for source code]

Use of AI services as part of the ACME product is governed by vendor contracts and the Vendor Management Policy, not this section.

## 6. Incident Reporting

Employees who believe their device or accounts have been compromised, or who observe a potential security violation, must report it immediately per the Incident Response Policy. Accidental violations of this policy are not grounds for discipline when promptly and honestly reported.

## 7. Personal Use of ACME Services

Employees may use ACME's own product for personal and business purposes within the same terms offered to customers, unless the {{SECURITY_OWNER}} specifies otherwise.

## 8. Violations

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Violations that result in a security incident are handled per the Incident Response Policy.
