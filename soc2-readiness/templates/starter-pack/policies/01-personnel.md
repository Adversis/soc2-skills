# Personnel Security Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** {{SECURITY_OWNER}}  
**Applies to:** All ACME employees and contractors

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes baseline security requirements for personnel at ACME AI, Inc., covering pre-employment screening, security awareness training, and annual policy acknowledgment. Security oversight for the company is held by the {{SECURITY_OWNER}}, with support from the founders. The {{SECURITY_OWNER}} approves and maintains this policy set.

## 2. Pre-Employment Screening

**Employees:** Before granting access to ACME systems or data, new employees complete security screening — [identity confirmation and reference checks, per company practice]{.cmt note="State the real process and retain what it produces (e.g., reference-check notes) in the personnel file or {{GRC_PLATFORM}}; the auditor will sample files. Work authorization (I-9, and E-Verify if used) is a separate legal employment step handled per applicable law — keep it out of the security access-approval gate; E-Verify in particular is opened after the I-9 and cannot be used pre-employment."}. Screening outputs are retained on file. Work-authorization is verified separately as a legal employment requirement, not as a security access gate.

**Contractors:** Before granting access to ACME systems or data, contractors must complete reference or background verification appropriate to the engagement, as determined by the {{SECURITY_OWNER}}.

ACME does not currently conduct formal criminal background checks; this will be revisited as the company grows or as customer contracts require it.

## 3. Security Awareness Training

All employees and contractors must complete security awareness training:

- **At onboarding:** Within the first two weeks of starting
- **Annually:** By the end of each calendar year

Training covers: phishing recognition, password hygiene, incident reporting, acceptable use of company systems, and handling of sensitive customer data.

Completion is recorded in the [Security Training Record]{.cmt note="tracked in {{GRC_PLATFORM}} (training completion module). Export per-employee completion dates before the audit"} (maintained by the {{SECURITY_OWNER}}).

## 4. Annual Security Review

All employees participate in an annual review that includes:

- Review of this policy set and any updates
- Acknowledgment that they have read and understood the Acceptable Use Policy
- Discussion of any security incidents or near-misses from the prior year
- Discussion of individual performance and contributions relative to role expectations

## 5. Access Provisioning and Deprovisioning

Access is provisioned and deprovisioned following the Onboarding and Offboarding Checklists (maintained separately). See the Access Control Policy for specific timelines and requirements.

## 6. Reporting Security Concerns

Employees are required to report suspected security incidents, policy violations, or concerns to the {{SECURITY_OWNER}} or via [{{SECURITY_CONTACT}}](mailto:{{SECURITY_CONTACT}}). There is no retaliation for good-faith reports.

## 7. Code of Conduct

ACME expects all personnel to:

- Act with honesty and integrity in business dealings
- Comply with applicable laws and ACME policies
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect; harassment and discrimination are not tolerated
- Protect ACME and customer information and avoid conflicts of interest
- Raise concerns about ethics, security, or compliance without fear of retaliation

This Code is acknowledged as part of the annual security review described in §4. Material violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
